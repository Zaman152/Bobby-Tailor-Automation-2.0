<objective>
Lock Phase 24's acceptance as one free command, modelled on
`tests/test_phase23_acceptance.py`.

Purpose: until this module exists, this phase's acceptance lives in summary documents. A summary
does not survive — it cannot be re-run, it cannot fail, and six months from now nobody can tell
which of its claims still hold. Phase 23 solved this by turning every acceptance criterion into
a committed command, and inheriting that module's three rules verbatim:

1. **Lock what SHIPPED, not what the plans predicted.** This phase has already falsified two
   research literals during planning, and execution will likely falsify more. Where a criterion
   was not delivered as written, the gate says so BY NAME and asserts the contract that was —
   it never quietly drops a check.
2. **Assert BANDS, POSITIONS and SETS — never a golden total.** Nothing here may assert equality
   against a golden bollard count, a golden column count, a golden wall area or an accuracy
   percentage. Tuning anything until a total matches is the coincidence trap this project has
   already paid days for.
3. **A check either ASSERTS or SKIPS.** No check may pass because its fixture is missing.

**Two research literals already falsified, which this gate must record rather than repeat:**

- **Open Question 4 answered NO.** A free deterministic probe over all four Crow sheets found the
  56-count and the highest-count symbol candidates carry ZERO contained text. Of 107 symbol
  groups, 81 have no contained text at all, 26 have text that is entirely drafting noise, and
  **none** carries a genuine object name. The research assumed contained text would be the
  preferred distance-zero anchor source; on the mission's primary plan set it contributes
  nothing.
- **Assumption A1 falsified.** The research attributed the 184-to-64 line-item swing to the
  un-ensembled pass. The saved artifacts show run 2 ran at a budget cap 2.5 times smaller,
  reached 99.8% of it, and spent less than half as much on sheet-level work. Budget, not
  sampling, is the dominant explanation.

**And one criterion that must be reframed, not asserted as written.** ROADMAP criterion 5 says
"a determinism harness runs the same fixture N times and asserts identical line items." That
formulation is vacuous against the replay analyzer, which is a pure function of its key. Plan
24-09 delivered the two-variant differential that actually detects the bug. This gate asserts the
delivered contract and names the reframing explicitly, per rule 1.

**Also assert what must not be lost.** The two saved paid runs under `.planning/paid-run-evidence/`
cost real money, are the only evidence of the variance this phase exists to kill, and are the
only seed material for the determinism fixtures. A previous commit purged roughly 1.1 GB from the
output directory. The gate asserts they are present.

Output: one test module; one command; the phase's acceptance made re-runnable.
</objective>
