<objective>
Bind a keynote callout to its own legend by the drawing region it sits in, so a sheet carrying two
legends over one number space can be read at all.

Purpose: Bob's page 4 carries 22 of the set's 31 keynote entries, including the three golden items
the engine cannot otherwise reach. Two legends there both number 01-06, four symbol groups tie at
six matched numbers, and 29-15 correctly refuses the page rather than pick by list order. The tie
is not noise to be broken — it is real, and the drawing resolves it by POSITION: a demolition
callout is drawn on the demolition elevation.
</objective>

<tasks>

## T-30-01-01 — Regions, from the sheet's own titles

`elevation_titles(page)` already returns the four titled regions. Derive each region's extent the
way the fill/measure routes already do, so one definition of "which rectangle is this drawing"
serves both. Do NOT invent a second region model.

## T-30-01-02 — Assign a legend to a region

A legend claims the region whose title it belongs to. Match on the legend header's own words
against the region title — DEMO KEYED NOTES to FRONT ELEVATION DEMOLITION — using only tokens both
strings actually print. Where a header matches no region, or matches more than one, that legend is
UNBOUND and its numbers are not counted from position.

## T-30-01-03 — Count per region

For each bound legend, count only the callout instances whose centres fall inside its region.
Instances outside every region are not counted at all.

The existing routes stay primary: enclosure first (a bubbled legend), then text cover (29-15), and
region binding only when a page carries MORE THAN ONE legend and the earlier routes refused.
