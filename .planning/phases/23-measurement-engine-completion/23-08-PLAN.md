<objective>
Wire the census and the finish tags into the pipeline: remove the single largest human input from the
Moxy path (SP-10), and turn ten absent wall-covering quantities into ten named flagged rows a human
can close in seconds (gap 5).

Purpose: `_manifest_room_mix`'s docstring asserts the unit multiplier is routinely not in the drawing
set and that no reading of the plan can recover it. That is FALSE, and correcting it is part of D-01.
Two stacked unit key plans carry 217 room tags with 217 distinct numbers, and counting them blind
reproduces four of four golden multipliers. Separately, every wall-covering quantity lives on an
interior-elevation sheet, those sheets have no plan titles at all, and the measurement loop therefore
never executes on the sheets holding the entire quantity. Today the pipeline emits NOTHING for them —
the worst of the three possible states, because silence is indistinguishable from zero.

Per D-10 the census SUPERSEDES the manifest where it has a count, the manifest is retained as a
cross-check that FLAGS disagreement, and `human_inputs/moxy_knoxville.json` is NOT edited in this
phase.

Output: `_resolve_room_mix` with per-count provenance, the material-tag bundle key, the flagged-row
binder, and the finish-schedule unit repair.
</objective>
