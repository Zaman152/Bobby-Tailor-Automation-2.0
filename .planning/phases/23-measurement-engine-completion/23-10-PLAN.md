Build the ONE open-polyline run chainer (SP-5 core) that gaps 4 and 6 both need, and unblock the
viewport that holds four of Crow's five rail marks.

must be one module (D-03). Neither can reuse the wall pairer: it requires two parallel faces at a
three-to-sixteen-inch offset, so a single-stroke pipe run can never be emitted, and the wall segment

contains an in-drawing note referring to a door-opening schedule — and that kind is masked out of
measurement AND barred from owning a printed scale, so four printed labels on that page bind to
nothing.

that sit on top are plan 23-12.
