<objective>
Replace the fixed clip box with a geometric, viewport-bounded clip cell (SP-4), and replace the
guest-room-sized plausibility threshold with the drawing's own printed extent (V4-MEAS-02).

Purpose: the clip is a fixed box hung off a title bounding box with NO knowledge of the drawing's
extent. It survives only by accident — adjacent clips overlap between eight and forty-eight percent
on every one of the sixteen prototype sheets, and correctness depends on leaked neighbours happening
to touch the clip edge. It COLLAPSES to a thirty-three-point strip on the level-seven reflected
ceiling plan, whose title sits near the page top, so that sheet measures nothing at all.

Read D-01 and INV-02 before starting: the widely-repeated claim that the clip leaks into an adjacent
plan on two guestroom sheets is FALSE and was falsified by three independent probes. Those two sheets
are bit-identical across a two-hundred-forty-point range of clip widths, every counted region sits
inside forty-seven percent of the clip, and the fill matches the drawing's OWN printed overall
dimensions at a ratio of one point zero zero seven. The real defect is the multiplier, which plan
23-13 makes visible. This plan still fixes the clip — because it is genuinely fragile and because
lobbies, ballrooms and elevations need it — but as a robustness and generality fix, not as the fix
for the two-times symptom.

Output: `clip_cells`, ink-extent tightening, the printed-extent bound and cross-check, the deletion
of the fixed clip constants and the guest-room plausibility constant, and clip-invariance proof.
</objective>
