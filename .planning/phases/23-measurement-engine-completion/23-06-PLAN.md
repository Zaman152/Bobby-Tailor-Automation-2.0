<objective>
Remove BOTH root causes of the silent sheet drop, structurally (D-04). The skip veto delivered in
plan 23-01 is a backstop; this plan removes the reasons it has to fire.

Purpose: two independent defects discard sixteen Moxy guestroom prototypes. First, the bare title
keyword is trusted inside the title-block crop and matches a word inside an electrical keynote
phrase, so `_KW_TITLE_BLOCK_ONLY` fires on a floor plan (INV-08 §1.2). Second,
`get_title_block_text` takes everything right of 55 percent of the page width, which on a
3024-point sheet swallows the adjacent keynote column so eighty-four words of prose outvote the
handful of title words (INV-00 §1c). The sheet identifier and the unit name are present in BOTH
windows — the classifier is not failing to see the title, it is being outvoted by prose. That makes
this a signal-to-noise defect, which is why the whole-word repair could not catch it.

**Neither may be fixed by retuning a constant.** Sweeping the width fraction from 0.55 to 0.88 fixes
Moxy from eighteen skips to two and BREAKS Chelsea from two to ten, collapsing its floor plans from
nine to one (INV-00 §1d). Any plan that changes that number is wrong.

Output: structural title-block identification, a keyword-context guard, and the four-plan-set gate
tightened to measure classification alone.
</objective>
