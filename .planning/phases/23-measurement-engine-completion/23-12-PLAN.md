    following INV-04 §3 steps one through nine exactly.

    Harvest through the styled harvester. Separate micro-strokes below the micro-length threshold and
    union-find them by endpoint proximity into blobs. A MARK is a blob whose stroke count lies in the
    observed band and whose maximum bounding span is under the glyph span cap; its SIGNATURE is the
    stroke count together with the rounded major and minor spans — a GROUPING key, never a name.
    Group the remaining segments by angle bin and perpendicular offset, project and merge with the
    gap tolerance.

    For each merged span and each signature, take the marks within the perpendicular tolerance of the
    line and inside the span, and require ALL FOUR gates: at least three marks of ONE signature; a
    mean pitch above the floor AND at most six times the glyph span; a pitch coefficient of variation
    at or below the cap; and at least eighty percent of marks BREAKING the line, meaning no ink under
    the mark and ink present on both sides within a fraction of the pitch.

    The pitch-over-span gate and the break-fraction gate are what separate a LINETYPE from a row of
    repeated tags sitting on a line. Do not loosen either.

    Clamp the run to the marks plus one pitch, intersected with the merged span. Deduplicate
    near-identical parallel runs of the same signature. Absorb, with ONE hop only, short stroked
    segments with a free endpoint near a run endpoint — terminal drops, risers and elbows carry no
    glyph and are short.

    Ship one hop and accept the residual. Do not widen to two hops.
  </action>
