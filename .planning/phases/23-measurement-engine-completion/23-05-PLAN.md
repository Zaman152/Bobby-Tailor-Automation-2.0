    Create `src/bobbytailor/deterministic/tag_pairing.py` exposing `project_bbox(bbox, text_dir)` and
    a generic `pair_two_token_tags(lines, first_pred, second_pred, max_gap_pt, perp_tol_pt)`.

    `project_bbox` maps a bounding box onto the text line's OWN direction axis, returning start, end,
    and the perpendicular low and high bounds, for all four cardinal directions: left-to-right uses x
    forward and y perpendicular; right-to-left negates and swaps the x bounds; the two vertical
    directions swap the roles of x and y, negating where the direction is negative.

    `pair_two_token_tags` walks candidate line records of text, bbox and direction; a pair is a line
    satisfying the first predicate followed by a line satisfying the second predicate where BOTH
    directions are equal, the gap between the first line's projected end and the second's projected
    start is non-negative and below the maximum, and the perpendicular ranges overlap within the
    tolerance. Pin the maximum gap at 16 points and the perpendicular tolerance at 3 points, each
    with a comment giving the observed value it was read from and the geometric reason for its
    margin — these come from the measured Moxy tag layouts in INV-05 §2 Key A, NOT from any quantity.

    This helper is the load-bearing piece of both remaining tasks and of the keynote pairing in plan
    23-14. Do not let any consumer grow its own copy.
  </action>
