<objective>
Kill the phantom linear-foot rows and bind measured runs to line items (gaps 4 and 6, SP-7).

Purpose: the non-zero rail figure the pipeline reports today is not a measurement of anything. It is
the rail's PIPE DIAMETER IN INCHES, read off a detail sheet as a dimension annotation and pushed
through a length branch that IGNORES the unit argument entirely, so an inch-and-a-half diameter
becomes about one and a half linear feet. The rounded formula string actively hides the unit error in
the audit trail. Seven such rows exist in the saved run, every one a pipe diameter, every one from
the details sheet — and the detail-sheet guard that would have caught them covers five other item
types and not rails.

Meanwhile basis `linear` has NO CONSUMER AT ALL, even though the component library already names both
rails correctly with the right basis and unit from the sheet's own keynotes. That is the exact shape
this phase exists to close: the name existed, the unit existed, the measurement step did not exist, so
it reported roughly zero.

Per D-06 and D-09 the numbers get WORSE here, deliberately and correctly: rails go from a confidently
wrong figure to a per-instance measurement, flagged, with the instance count unknown. Ship the item
flagged rather than holding it back — a withheld item is a silent zero, which this phase exists to
abolish.

Output: unit-aware formulas, the widened detail guard, the linear binder, library-driven basis
classification, the ambiguity gate, and one canonical name form.
</objective>
