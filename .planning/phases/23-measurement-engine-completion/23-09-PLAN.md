<objective>
Build the region-geometry primitive (SP-3): one shared mask labeller, an outer-contour trace, a
closed-ring-safe simplifier, and perimeter with a blind shape ratio.

Purpose: wall area needs perimeter times height, and the perimeter primitive does not exist anywhere
in the package — `_label_regions` builds a labelled bitmap and DISCARDS it, returning only area,
pixel count and an edge-touch flag. The probe-confirmed result field list carries no perimeter and no
contour. Two hazards are already proven and must live in the shared code rather than being
rediscovered per gap: plain simplification of a ring whose endpoints coincide returns zero length,
and raw raster boundary counting over-reads by roughly a factor of six. INV-05 measured the naive
method precisely to show it must not be shipped; INV-01 shipped the traced method and measured it at
under one and a half percent from the raw trace.

Output: the labeller, the trace, the ring-safe simplifier, perimeter and shape ratio on both
dataclasses and in the serialised form, plus the linear scale factor beside the existing area factor.
</objective>
