<objective>
Make the corroboration verdict 28-01 recorded actually decide what publishes.

Purpose: `Stairs` published `MEASURED`, `needs_review=False`, confidence medium, at 1.00 in run A
and 2.00 in run B. Every other wrong number in that report was at least flagged — this one was not,
and it was also the one that moved between two identical invocations. V4-FIX-01 is the phase's first
duty because an unflagged wrong number is worse than a blank: a reviewer skips it.

Two rules land here, in the layer that owns the four-value client vocabulary:

1. **Withhold, don't publish.** A count whose only evidence is a typical-details sheet becomes a
   BLANK with its reason. The reading is quoted in the reason so nothing is deleted.
2. **Flag whatever is left.** Any surviving count with no printed table and no geometry behind it
   publishes `NEEDS REVIEW`, never `MEASURED`.

Output: the publish decision, made once, in the place that already knows the difference between a
blank and a zero.
</objective>
