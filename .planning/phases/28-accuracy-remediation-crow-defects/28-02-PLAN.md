<objective>
Stop `perimeter_jamb_symbol` from naming a cluster that is not at a perimeter and not at a jamb.

Purpose: `Bollards 56 EA` against golden 28 looks like a textbook double-count — exactly 2x, one
detail line, `formula: count`, identical in both paid runs. **It is not a double-count.** Free
position evidence over the real Crow PDF, taken before this plan was written, falsifies that
hypothesis outright. The clean 2x is a coincidence, and acting on it by halving would be the
documented coincidence trap in its purest form.

What is actually wrong is a mis-binding: the naming library's `perimeter_jamb_symbol` hint was
attached to a cluster whose instances form a regular interior lattice on the structural bay grid,
because `_is_perimeter_ring` cannot tell a hollow ring from a coarse lattice, and because a
count-tie between two clusters was broken by list order.

Output: an honest ring test, a binding that refuses rather than guesses, and a committed
falsification of the doubling hypothesis so nobody re-derives it by spending API.
</objective>
