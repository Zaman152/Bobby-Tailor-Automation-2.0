<objective>
Turn 209 EA of measured-but-unnameable geometry from dead weight into one-click calibration, by
asking the human a question the app is not entitled to answer.

Purpose: the app measures far more than it can name. Those marks are counted, located and
croppable; the only thing missing is what they are, and nothing printed on the sheet says. Today
they sit in a ledger nobody acts on. A named cluster is a priced line item; an unnamed one is
nothing, however accurately it was counted.

This is the first change in the phase that targets the measured bottleneck rather than a
suspected one. It also makes 29-10's loop contribute for the first time: 39 of its 46 needs stop
being inert.
</objective>

<tasks>

## T-29-11-01 — The offer

`NamingOffer(candidate_id, sheet, quantity, unit, position_pt, manifest_name, why)`.

`why` states the ONLY grounds that produced it — matching unit and measure basis — in words that
do not overclaim: "36 counted marks on A-101; your manifest lists Columns as EA/count. Confirm
only if that is what these are."

An offer is NOT a resolution. It never sets a name, never clears `needs_review`, and never
appears in `takeoff_summary`.

## T-29-11-02 — Eligibility, and nothing more

An entry is offered ONLY when unit matches AND measure basis matches. No token overlap, no fuzzy
score, no position heuristic, no "grid intersections are columns". Those are the shape-naming
moves the anchored-only rule exists to forbid, and adding one here would smuggle it back in
through a surface the human is likely to trust.

## T-29-11-03 — Ambiguity shows

Three eligible entries produce three offers against one cluster, ordered but with NO default and
no ranking implied. `offers_for(candidate)` returns them all. Nothing anywhere picks one.

## T-29-11-04 — Declines persist

A declined `(project, candidate signature, manifest_name)` triple is recorded so the next run of
the same project does not re-ask an identical question. Accepts already persist through the
learning store; declines must too, or calibration becomes Sisyphean.

## T-29-11-05 — Tests

- an unnamed EA/count cluster is offered an EA/count manifest entry, with its `why`
- an SF/area entry is NEVER offered against an EA/count cluster
- three eligible entries produce three offers and no default
- an offer changes NOTHING: `takeoff_summary`, quantities, `needs_review` and coverage are
  byte-identical with offers present and absent
- accepting an offer names the row and records HUMAN INPUT provenance, never MEASURED
- a declined offer is not re-raised for the same project
- a run with no manifest raises no offers at all
- NON-VACUITY: every assertion proved red before being kept

</tasks>

- An offer NEVER becomes a quantity, a coverage percentage, or an accuracy number. A run's score
  must be identical whether offers were generated or not — asserted, not assumed.
- Does NOT close Crow's Columns to 132. The 120 measured marks may be offered the name "Columns";
  the count remains 120 and remains flagged.
- If the four-set sweep shows offers being generated in volumes a human would not read (hundreds
  per sheet), CAP and report rather than shipping a wall of questions.
</limits>
