<objective>
Stop the last route that turns an unevidenced guess into a published wall area.

Purpose: `CMU Wall` is the worst row in the Crow report — 40,086 SF against a golden of 2,204, an
1,718% error. Every guard upstream worked. 28-04's mutual-uniqueness test refused BOTH thickness
binds as contested. The region route's answer was rejected. And then a binding hint — a rule whose
whole evidence is "masonry is small" — supplied a number anyway, and the report published it.

The drawing itself disagrees, in print: A-401 says `8" CMU wall - section at pump/electrical
room`. CMU on this building is one room. The engine measured a building.

Output: that row becomes a flagged blank quoting the 40,086 SF it refused and naming the hint that
produced it. The estimator loses nothing they could have used — a number 18x wrong is not a
starting point — and gains a row that tells them exactly what to measure.
</objective>

<tasks>

## T-29-12-01 — Identify the hint-only wall area

A calculated row is hint-bound when its wall class was named by `_NAME_SOURCE_LIBRARY` — the
binding-hint route — with no thickness match and no text anchor. Use the marker fusion already
sets; do not re-derive the condition from names or numbers.

Scope it to AREA rows (`wall_area` basis / SF). A hint-bound COUNT is a different question with
its own guards, and widening here would be the over-reach 28-03 refused.

## T-29-12-02 — Publish the blank, quoting what it withheld

Register a new marker in `_UNMEASURED_SOURCES` beside `UNIT_CLASS_CONFLICT_UNMEASURED` and
`DETAIL_SHEET_COUNT_UNMEASURED`, and materialise a ROW the same way — a withheld number that
leaves no row is a deletion, and a deletion and a zero read identically to a client (the exact bug
9e95b56 fixed elsewhere).

The reason states: the withheld quantity, that the class was named by a binding hint and nothing
printed, and — where the run captured one — the sheet text that scopes the material differently.
On Crow that is A-401's pump/electrical room section.

## T-29-12-03 — Prove the authoritative routes untouched

A thickness-matched bind (`naming_library_thickness`), a text-anchored bind, a manifest-named
region area and a printed-schedule area must all publish exactly as they do today. Assert each
directly rather than trusting that the marker is narrow.

Crow's `Tilt Up Wall` at 106,992 SF (1.34% error) rides an authoritative route and is the
regression canary: if it moves, the marker is too wide.
