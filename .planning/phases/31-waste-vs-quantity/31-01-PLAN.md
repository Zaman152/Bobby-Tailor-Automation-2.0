<objective>
Publish the measured quantity, and carry waste as a separate recorded fact.

Purpose: the app inflates 16 item types by 3-12% above what any estimator's take-off states, so it
can never match one on those rows — and the error is small enough to read as measurement noise
rather than as a modelling decision. Gas Piping is the proof: 3.9% error, of which 5 points are a
waste factor we add and 1 point is real.
</objective>

<tasks>
## T-31-01-01 — Quantity is the measurement
`_apply_formula` publishes the measured value. `waste_factor` stays on the row, unchanged, as its
own field. Nothing is deleted.

## T-31-01-02 — Say so on the row
Where a waste factor was recorded but not applied, the row's formula text states the measured basis
and names the factor, so a reader can price it without re-deriving anything.

## T-31-01-03 — Measure the blast radius, all four sets
Report every row that moves, per set, before/after. A change this wide must be seen, not assumed.

## T-31-01-04 — Tests
- Gas Piping publishes 877.60, not 921.48, and records waste 1.05
- a table with NO waste factor is byte-identical
- the four-set census committed as a pin
- NON-VACUITY: proved red first
</tasks>

<limits>
- Does NOT change any waste FACTOR value. Whether 1.05 is right for gas pipe is a separate question;

- Expect golden scores to MOVE ON EVERY SET. Rows currently failing high may pass; any row that was
  accidentally passing BECAUSE of the inflation will now fail, and that is the correct outcome.
